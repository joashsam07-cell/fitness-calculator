About the Fitness & Body Composition Calculator

This comprehensive health analysis tool evaluates essential body metrics including Body Mass Index (BMI), Body Fat Percentage, Waist-to-Hip Ratio (WHR), and Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR) to provide personalized fitness guidance.

Health Metrics Overview

  • BMI (Body Mass Index): Measures relative body mass based on height and weight.
  • Body Fat Percentage: Utilizes U.S. Navy tape measure formulas to estimate overall body fat vs. lean mass.
  • Waist-to-Hip Ratio (WHR): Assesses central body fat accumulation and related metabolic risk factors.
  • Waist-to-Height Ratio (WHtR): Monitors visceral abdominal fat proportions for cardiovascular health evaluation.
