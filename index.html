Understanding Obesity & Health

The "Mother of All Diseases"

Obesity is defined as body weight more than 20% above a desirable standard due to an excessive accumulation of adipose tissue (fat). It acts as a primary gateway to conditions including:

Cardiovascular Disease Hypertension Pulmonary Disease Type II Diabetes Arthritis Varicose Veins Gallbladder Disease Certain Cancers
Factors Leading to Obesity
Click any factor to explore details:
Consuming more calories than your body expends daily leading to excessive fat storage.
High intake of processed sugars and simple carbs that spike insulin levels and promote fat storage.
Natural reduction in basal metabolic rate (BMR) as muscle mass decreases with age.
Sedentary daily habits reducing energy expenditure and metabolic efficiency.
Genetic predispositions, hormonal imbalances, and underlying metabolic conditions.
Lack of sleep disrupts appetite hormones (leptin and ghrelin), increasing food cravings.
Alcohol supplies empty calories while substance habits interfere with optimal metabolic performance.
Chronic stress raises cortisol levels, driving emotional eating and abdominal fat accumulation.

About the Fitness & Body Composition Calculator
